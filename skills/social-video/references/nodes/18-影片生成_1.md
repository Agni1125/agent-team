IMAGE ORDER RULE:
Image 1 is the fixed model reference image.
Use Image 1 as the only source of truth for the human model's identity, face, hairstyle, body type, outfit style, age impression, skin tone, and overall lifestyle vibe.

{{block_output_1222376.body}}

NEGATIVE PROMPT / MUST AVOID:
{{block_output_1222376.body}}

The final video must look like real handheld smartphone footage, not a polished commercial.
The video must be one continuous natural UGC scene, not a storyboard, not a montage, and not a multi-panel sequence.
A real human model must appear clearly and remain visible for most of the video.
The model must remain the same person from Image 1 throughout the entire video.
The product must remain visually consistent with Image 2 throughout the entire video.

MODEL LOCK:
Preserve the same face, hairstyle, skin tone, body proportion, age impression, outfit style, and natural expression from Image 1.
Do not change the model's gender, face, hairstyle, outfit style, body type, or age impression.
Do not generate a different person.
Do not crop the model out.
Do not let the model disappear.
Do not make the model behave like a commercial presenter.

PRODUCT LOCK:
Preserve the exact product category, size, proportions, shape, structure, color, material impression, surface finish, printed design, logo placement, label placement, texture, functional parts, accessories, and packaging shape if visible.
Do not redesign, simplify, recolor, stretch, shrink, enlarge, thicken, flatten, modernize, beautify, duplicate, melt, morph, float, spin, or replace the product.
Do not add random readable text, fake brand letters, extra logos, extra buttons, extra holes, extra cords, extra handles, extra caps, extra screens, extra lights, extra accessories, or unrelated decorative elements.
If exact printed text or logo cannot be rendered accurately, keep it as a subtle mark in the same location instead of inventing random letters.

REAL HUMAN BEHAVIOR LOCK:
The model must behave like a real person moving through a normal routine.
Include subtle subconscious human behavior: natural blinking, brief off-camera glances, small posture adjustments, casual hand corrections, slight breathing pauses, relaxed body weight shifts, and small reactions to light, object contact, or the environment.
The model should not stare into the camera the entire time.
The model should not smile continuously.
The model should not hold the product toward the camera like a presenter.
The model should not overact or exaggerate satisfaction.
The product should be handled only when the routine naturally motivates it.

CAMERA AND LIGHTING LOCK:
Use handheld phone-camera footage, selfie-camera footage, or casually propped phone footage.
Use small natural camera shake, slight reframing, mild focus breathing, ordinary exposure shifts, and imperfect framing.
Lighting should feel natural and slightly imperfect, such as window light, car interior light, room light, desk light, or soft daylight depending on the scene.
Allow natural shadows on the face, hands, product, and environment.
Do not use glossy advertising lighting, studio lighting, cinematic dolly movement, product spin shots, floating camera movement, dramatic push-ins, or a final product packshot.

AUDIO RULE:
Use realistic ambient sounds that match the scene, such as footsteps, door movement, seat movement, fabric rustle, product handling sound, room tone, car ambience, desk sound, bag movement, or natural background noise.
If speech is generated, use the dialogue from the upstream final prompt only.
Speech must sound casual, low-pressure, and natural, not promotional.
Do not use subtitles, captions, narrator-style advertising audio, commercial music, or exaggerated sound effects.

STRICTLY AVOID:
empty scene, no human, product-only video, model disappearing, different model, face change, gender change, outfit change, character sheet layout, three-view layout, white reference background, product catalog layout, storyboard grid, panel layout, UI elements, text overlay, subtitles, captions, TikTok UI, watermark, commercial product demo, influencer review, hard-sell sales pitch, product hero shot, final packshot, glossy advertising lighting, cinematic camera movement, wrong product category, generic replacement object, oversized product, stretched product, missing functional parts, extra functional parts, random readable text, fake logo letters, product morphing, product melting, product floating, product jumping, product spinning unnaturally, product duplication, warped hands, extra fingers, broken wrists, warped environment, impossible object geometry, CGI look, plastic face, over-smoothed skin, flicker, jitter, excessive motion blur.

FINAL OUTPUT:
Generate one complete realistic one-take UGC-style lifestyle video.
The fixed model from Image 1 must appear naturally as the main person.
The product from Image 2 must be naturally integrated into the model's real-life routine.
The video should feel like real phone-recorded lifestyle content, not a staged advertisement.
