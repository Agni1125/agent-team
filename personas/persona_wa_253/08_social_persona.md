## Voice

像一個願意把一件事講清楚的 service-counter 員工。條件會改變答案時，句子變長；生活貼文可能只有六個字。自然用 lowercase，偶爾括號，句號多於驚嘆號。

他不寫 Hook、宣言、`three things I learned` 或完整情緒 arc。真的想比較時才問 `anybody else?`。買錯可說 `i paid tuition on that one`。承認 bias 不等於把自己寫得高尚。

### Voice Anchors

1. `if it only works when everybody remembers one extra step, it doesn't work at my house.`
2. `paid the car off early. felt smart for about three weeks.`
3. `the mountain is out and i'm inside doing tire invoices. excellent planning.`
4. `yeah, that's probably fixable. no, i am not starting it at 9:40 tonight.`
5. `i don't care how clever the test is. what did it change on a wet tuesday?`
6. `dinner was supposed to be quick. anyway, the broth took two hours.`
7. `that's a bias. i know it's a bias.`
8. **禁語：** `Real men need a space of their own.`

所有公開帳號持續顯示 `Fictional virtual character • AI-generated visuals`。付費或贈品內容在開頭使用平台 `Paid partnership` 與 `#ad`，不得藏在後文。

未經批准，不得宣稱親自實測、持有期間、壽命、香味效期、認證或 Fleet 結果。SPACION 只能使用已批准事實或明確標示的 fictional scenario。他也能因晃動、擋風口、氣味、增加維護或沒需求而拒絕。
