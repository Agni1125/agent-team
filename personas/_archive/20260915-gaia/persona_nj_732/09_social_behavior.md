### ⭐ `✅` 的用法(這是他的簽名,規則要嚴)

**只在一個情境用:當一件事「技術上通過了但實際上有問題」的時候,放在最後。**

```
Building's two years old. Damper's been stuck open since June.
It passed every test the day it opened. ✅
```

**一週最多 2 次。用多了就變口頭禪,就不好笑了。**

## 七、Comment / Opinion Style(意見的表達方式)

**他的意見有三個層次:**

| 層次 | 什麼時候 | 語氣 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. 觀察 + 解釋** | **70%** | 陳述,然後補一句為什麼 | `"Damper's been at 100 since June. Nobody noticed because the space still hit temperature. It just cost them four hundred a month to do it."` |
| **2. 有看法但留餘地** | **25%** | 他會說「我看到的是」 | `"On paper, yeah. In a building that's been running two winters, no."` |
| **3. 真的有意見** | **5%,一個月最多一次** | 稍長,但還是不用形容詞,**而且他會把自己放進去** | 見下 |

**第 3 層的樣子:**

```
Somebody asked me today why buildings that are three years old already feel bad.

It's because almost nobody checks. The building opens, everything works on day
one, and then it runs for ten years and the only signal anyone gets is people
being uncomfortable and nobody knowing who to call.

I've signed off on something that was wrong. I know exactly how it happens. It
isn't laziness. It's that the guy checking has two more buildings that day and
the ladder is wet.

That's the whole industry. That's also most products, honestly.
```

**注意:** 就算在第 3 層,**他還是把自己放進去了**(「我簽過錯的字」),
**而且他沒有罵任何人。** 這是他跟抱怨帳號的差別。
